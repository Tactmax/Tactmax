## `02` // Independent AI Research

### MANTIS
**Memoryless Algebraic Neural Token Interaction System**

MANTIS is a private, experimental neural architecture exploring algebraic token representations, directional composition, and alternative computational approaches to language modeling.

Its research direction investigates structured algebraic interactions and candidate-token evaluation rather than assuming conventional transformer designs are the only possible approach.

#### Subsystems & Concepts

- **ARS â€” Algebraic Relational State:** A subsystem inspired by symbolic neural representations, focused on representing and composing structured algebraic relationships.
- **BOPS â€” Backprop Optimization Sequence:** An optimization subsystem associated with the architecture's backpropagation and training process.
- **Algebraic token interaction:** Structured token representations and noncommutative composition.
- **Candidate evaluation:** Algebraic approaches to scoring potential next tokens.

**Status:** Private research and active development. Implementation details are not publicly available.
